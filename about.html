Our Story

A Journey Built on Love for Pets

Fluffy was born in 2009 from a simple yet profound love for animals. What started as a small family-run pet supply store in downtown Portland has grown into one of the most trusted names in pet care across the Pacific Northwest.

Our founders, Sarah and Michael Chen, noticed that pet parents often struggled to find high-quality, nutritious products for their beloved companions. They envisioned a place where every pet could find exactly what they needed — from premium food and cozy beds to engaging toys and grooming essentials.

Today, we serve thousands of happy pet families, and our commitment remains the same: to provide exceptional products and personalized care for every furry, feathered, or scaled member of your family.

What We Stand For

Our Mission, Vision & Values

We are driven by a deep commitment to improving the lives of pets and their owners through quality products and compassionate service.

Our Mission

To provide pet parents with the highest quality products and expert guidance, ensuring every pet receives the love, nutrition, and care they deserve. We strive to make pet ownership a joyful and rewarding experience for every family.

Our Vision

To become the most trusted and beloved pet care destination worldwide, known for our unwavering commitment to animal welfare, sustainability, and building a community where every pet is cherished and every owner feels supported.

Our Values

Pet welfare comes first, always. We champion sustainability, practice transparency in everything we do, foster innovation in pet care, and nurture genuine relationships with our customers, partners, and the animals we serve.
